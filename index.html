HOW TO SURVIVE

Crawlers walk toward you along both the floor and the ceiling. Touching one in any other way costs a mask.

To slay a foe you must land upon it from above. Flip your gravity, fall toward your target, and stomp it as you arrive. The same works in reverse — flip up and crash into a ceiling crawler from below.

The dash also kills, and its cooldown is shown as a thin teal bar beneath your knight.

You begin with three masks When all three shatter, the cavern claims you.
